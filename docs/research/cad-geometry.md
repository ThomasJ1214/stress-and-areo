# Research: cad-geometry

> **Topic:** cad-geometry: Python CAD/geometry stack for STEP/IGES/STL import, exact mass properties, watertightness/repair, outer-skin (OML) extraction, unit inference, alignment to OpenRocket components, sectional area distribution, booleans and mesh export (Windows, Python 3.12)

_Generated from a hands-on feasibility research agent (2026-10). Items marked VERIFIED were run/read by the agent; others are from recall and must be re-checked before relying on them._

## Recommendation

Use OpenCASCADE 8.0.1 through the pip package cadquery-ocp-novtk==8.0.1.1.0 (OCP bindings) as the single geometry kernel. Pick the novtk variant because plain cadquery-ocp pins vtk==9.6.2 exactly. Put a small in-house adapter around it (about 400 lines, prototyped in scripts/occ_util.py, xcaf_util.py, oml.py, sections.py and align.py) and route every OCP call through it, because OCP 8 broke several 7.x APIs. Licences: OCP is Apache-2.0 and OCCT is LGPL-2.1 with an exception; OCCT ships as separate DLLs, which is fine to distribute. OCP handles: STEP AP203/214/242 and IGES import via XCAF (part names, colours, assembly tree, instance transforms, automatic unit conversion to mm); BRepCheck validation; sewing/ShapeFix; exact B-rep mass properties (volume, CG and full 3x3 inertia tensor match analytic values to 1e-13..1e-15); multi-argument fuse; outer-shell OML extraction; exact planar sections; BRepMesh tessellation with per-face IDs; STEP/STL export; glTF export with names and per-face primitives (RWGltf_CafWriter). For mesh-only inputs (STL/OBJ/PLY/OFF/3MF/GLB) use trimesh (MIT) for IO, welding, watertight/Euler checks, polyhedral mass properties, small-hole repair and planform projections. Add manifold3d (Apache-2.0) for fast slicing (5-10x faster than trimesh) and mesh booleans, and shapely for 2-D section algebra. Use gmsh (GPL-2+) only for FEA tet meshing and CFD surface/volume meshing from the same STEP, with XCAF instance paths becoming physical groups. Run gmsh in a separate worker process/exe: gmsh.exe from the gmsh.info Windows SDK zip, or a separately frozen mesher. That keeps the main app's licences clean and matches the user's allowance. Do not use gmsh for display tessellation: it took 12-116 s on a nose cone versus 0.03-4 s for BRepMesh, and hung at the apex without MeshSizeMin. Do not ship cascadio (a redundant second OCCT copy), numpy-stl (trimesh covers it), pythonocc-core (conda-only, not on PyPI) or build123d. build123d is only a convenience layer and pulls in ipython, sympy, scikit-learn and ezdxf, so keep it for dev/test fixtures. Do not use trimesh voxel OML (±8-10% volume error and out-of-memory at ≤1.5 mm pitch). Target Python 3.12: every candidate has cp312 win_amd64 wheels, numpy 2.5 and scipy 1.18 already dropped 3.11, and 3.13 wheels exist too.

Two architecture rules, both shown by tests here. (1) Do booleans and the OML in B-rep before tessellating. A mesh union of independently tessellated coincident curved faces gave the right volume (−3e-4) but the wrong topology (Euler χ=−16, genus 9) and +1.75% wetted area. The B-rep OML was exact: volume error 2e-15 and exact wetted area (74418.07 = 64364.98 + 10053.10 mm²). (2) Validate every import twice, with BRepCheck_Analyzer plus agreement between B-rep volume and tessellated-mesh volume, and round-trip-test every STEP the app writes. OCCT 8.0.1 wrote a STEP of a valid revolved hollow ogive that reads back invalid with 7x the volume; IGES of the same shape and a boolean-built version were both fine. Mass properties shown to users come from the B-rep, labelled 'exact geometry × assumed density'. Mesh-derived values carry the chordal error estimate (4/3)δ/r and are labelled as such.

Component replacement workflow: import and validate, then assign materials and compute B-rep mass properties (optionally with measured mass/CG overrides), then fit to the OR component, then remove the OR component's own mass and replace it with the CAD composite (m, c, full tensor), then build the OML for aero. The fit is: infer units for mesh inputs; get the axis from the inertia eigen-decomposition, refined by face-normal covariance; locate the axis by least squares on the normal lines with trimming; resolve direction by r(s) profile matching; take roll from the user; snap the fore face to OR x_fore; report ΔR/ΔL without auto-scaling. Double-counted OR subcomponents inside the CAD span are flagged for the user. OML: B-rep fuse of the leaf solids plus thin interface plugs, keep outer shells, drop internal solids, then fuse with OCP solids generated from the other OR components to get the whole-rocket OML for CFD. The engineering tier gets A_enc(x)/r_eq(x)/r_max(x), wetted area, planform areas, protuberance frontal area and a non-axisymmetry flag. The structures tier gets A_mat(x), Iyy(x) and Izz(x) of the load-carrying parts from exact B-rep sections. On synthetic fixtures with random pose and unknown units the alignment recovered the axis to ≤1.5e-6°, centring to 0.02 mm and axial placement exactly, and inferred the unit correctly 4/4 times. It resolved nose-cone direction (0.21 vs 22.7 mm RMS) and correctly flagged cylinder direction/roll as ambiguous.

- ANALYTIC REFERENCE EQUATIONS (scripts/analytic.py) [VERIFIED-RUN]. Convention: lengths in mm, rocket axis +X, inertia tensor I=[[Ixx,-Pxy,-Pxz],[-Pxy,Iyy,-Pyz],[-Pxz,-Pyz,Izz]] about the CG, with Ixx=∫(y²+z²)dV and Pxy=∫xy dV. This is the same as OCCT GProp MatrixOfInertia and gmsh getMatrixOfInertia, verified with the fin's non-zero product. Solid of revolution with y_i<=r<=y_o: V=π∫(y_o²−y_i²)dx; x̄=π∫x(y_o²−y_i²)dx/V; Ixx=(π/2)∫(y_o⁴−y_i⁴)dx; Iyy,origin=π∫[x²(y_o²−y_i²)+(y_o⁴−y_i⁴)/4]dx; Iyy=Izz=Iyy,origin−V x̄² (scipy quad epsrel 1e-13). Tangent ogive: ρ=(R²+L²)/(2R), y(x)=√(ρ²−(L−x)²)+R−ρ, x from the tip. Test L=240, R=40 gives V=648481.1054 mm³, x̄=164.554. Hollow variant uses y_i=y−t, t=2, giving V=77878.4935. Polygon fin with k_i=x_i y_{i+1}−x_{i+1}y_i: A=½Σk_i; Cx=Σ(x_i+x_{i+1})k_i/(6A); ∫x²dA=Σ(x_i²+x_i x_{i+1}+x_{i+1}²)k_i/12; ∫y²dA analogous; ∫xy dA=Σ(x_i y_{i+1}+2x_i y_i+2x_{i+1}y_{i+1}+x_{i+1}y_i)k_i/24. Plate of thickness t: Ixx,o=t∫y²dA+At³/12; Iyy,o=t∫x²dA+At³/12; Izz,o=t(∫x²+∫y²)dA; Pxy=t∫xy dA; I_cg=I_o−V(|c|²E−ccᵀ). Fin cr=120, ct=60, s=80, sweep 40, t=3.175: A=7200, V=22860, CG=(64.444, 35.556, 0). Tube: V=π(Ro²−Ri²)L, I_ax=V(Ro²+Ri²)/2, I_tr=V[3(Ro²+Ri²)+L²]/12. Box: I=V/12·diag(b²+c², a²+c², a²+b²). Saddle (camera shroud = box minus cylinder) with g=√(Ro²−y²): V=Lx∫(zt−g)dy, ∫z dV=Lx∫(zt²−g²)/2 dy, ∫z² dV=Lx∫(zt³−g³)/3 dy. Composite: m=Σm_i, c=Σm_i c_i/m, I=Σ[I_i+m_i(|d_i|²E−d_i d_iᵀ)].

- TESSELLATION [VERIFIED-RUN; out/test_tess.json]. Pipeline: OCCT BRepMesh_IncrementalMesh(shape, δ, False, ang, True), then per-face BRep_Tool.Triangulation (reverse the winding for REVERSED faces, apply the location), then trimesh weld. Ogive solid as δ goes 0.5 → 0.1 → 0.02 → 0.01 mm: 2395 → 11801 → 55889 → 111185 triangles; dV −9.0e-3 → −1.8e-3 → −3.7e-4 → −1.9e-4; dI 1.3e-2 → 2.7e-4; time 0.03 → 0.17 → 1.2 → 3.1 s. Tube δ 0.5 → 0.01: dV −2.6e-3 → −8.6e-5 in 3-33 ms, watertight. Fin: exact with 12 triangles. The ogive mesh is NOT watertight after welding only because of one degenerate triangle at the apex pole; after update_faces(nondegenerate_faces()) it is watertight. The pipeline must be: weld, drop degenerate faces, drop duplicates, then check watertight/winding/Euler. - CROSS-SECTIONS [VERIFIED-RUN; out/test_slice.json, out/payload_sections.csv]. Per station compute both A_enc (area enclosed by the outer contour; for aero slender-body dA/dx and r_eq=√(A_enc/π)) and A_mat, Iyy, Izz (material area and centroidal second moments; for structures). Ring formulas with k_i=y_i z_{i+1}−y_{i+1}z_i: A=½Σk_i; ∫z²dA=Σ(z_i²+z_i z_{i+1}+z_{i+1}²)k_i/12; Iyy=∫z²dA−A z_c². Exact B-rep method: cut with a half-space box, keep the planar faces on x=x_s, then BRepGProp.SurfaceProperties (∫dy²=M33, ∫dz²=M22). Ogive B-rep max 1.5e-5 (intersection-curve tolerance near the tip), 6.1 s; hollow ogive 1.5e-4, 12.1 s. trimesh at δ=0.02: max 3.6e-3, median 3.8e-4, 1.25 s (ogive). - OML (WETTED OUTER SKIN) EXTRACTION, B-REP [VERIFIED-RUN; scripts/oml.py, out/test_oml_align.json]. Algorithm: (1) add thin interface plugs at the component's fore and aft interface planes (bore-radius disks 0.5 mm thick), because the adjacent component closes the bore; (2) BRepAlgoAPI_Fuse with SetArguments([s0]) and SetTools([s1..sn]) (fuzzy value available), then ShapeUpgrade_UnifySameDomain; (3) take BRepClass3d.OuterShell_s for each solid and MakeSolid, which removes voids; (4) drop solids whose interior point lies inside a larger outer-shell solid (BRepClass3d_SolidClassifier), which removes floating internals such as the sled and battery; (5) the remaining solid is the OML, with faces on the interface planes tagged as non-wetted caps. Results: closed payload V error −2.4e-15, dCG 1e-13 mm, dI 1.7e-15, area 74418.07 (exact), 29 ms. Open tube without plugs: −82.6%, because the bore is counted as outer skin. Open tube with plugs: −2.4e-15, 23 ms. Output written to step/payload_oml.step (8 faces); tessellated it is watertight with χ=2.
- MESH-PATH OML AND BOOLEAN PITFALLS [VERIFIED-RUN]. Mesh path: manifold3d batch_boolean(Add) of 8 welded part meshes takes 3.7 ms. trimesh split, then drop slivers with |V|<1e-6·ΣV (13-24 slivers), drop negative-volume void shells, drop positive shells contained in others. Result: volume −3.2e-4 and watertight, BUT Euler χ=−16 (genus 9) and area +1.75% (75722 vs 74418 mm²). The bulkhead and bore polygons tessellate the same circle differently and leave sliver channels, so this is unusable for CFD and wetted area. - UNIT INFERENCE AND ALIGNMENT [VERIFIED-RUN; scripts/align.py]. Unit score S(u)=|ln(L_native·s_u/L_OR)|+|ln(R_native·s_u/R_OR)| over u ∈ {mm 1, cm 10, m 1000, in 25.4, ft 304.8}, accepted if S<2ln1.15. Axis: inertia eigenvectors, choosing the distinct eigenvalue (prolate if (λ2−λ1)/λ2 ≥ (λ3−λ2)/λ3), then refined as the smallest eigenvector of C=Σ A_i n_i n_iᵀ. This works for any surface of revolution while the mean of (n·a)² is below 1/3. Degenerate faces must be dropped first: the apex triangle produced NaNs. Axis point: least squares on normal lines, minimising Σ A_i|Q_i(x−p_i)|² with Q_i=P_a−n̂n̂ᵀ, with 25% trimmed refits to reject protrusions. Do NOT centre on the CG, which is legitimately 6 mm off-axis here. Test: the payload OML in random pose, exported to STL in in, m, mm and cm, then fitted to an OR tube (R40, L250, fore end at x=600). Units inferred 4/4; axis angle error ≤1.5e-6°; fore end at 600.000; L 250.0000x; R_fit 39.992 (−0.008 mm, the tessellation sagitta); radial centre offset 0.020 mm; ~20 ms; direction correctly flagged ambiguous. Ogive in inches, 3 random poses, matched against the OR r(s) profile: direction resolved (RMS 0.21 vs 22.68 mm); tip at x=0 with radial offset ≤0.004 mm; base r 40.005; L error 0.00025 mm.

- WHY CAD INERTIA MATTERS. The payload modelled OpenRocket-style as a body tube with mass overridden to 0.4952 kg, with inertia = OR geometric unit inertia × mass ([RECALL]; verify in OpenRocket's MassCalculation / getRotationalUnitInertia source): I_axial=m(Ro²+Ri²)/2=7.54e-4 kg·m², which is +52.8% vs the CAD value of 4.93e-4. I_transverse=m(3(Ro²+Ri²)+L²)/12=2.96e-3, +10.2% vs 2.68e-3. 6-DOF roll and pitch dynamics would be off by that much.

- COMPONENT REPLACEMENT WORKFLOW (design). Steps A-C: (A) Import and validate. Sniff the format; STEP/IGES go to XCAF (mm). Per leaf run BRepCheck, test closed shells, otherwise use a sewing tolerance ladder, MakeSolid and ShapeFix_Solid; if it is still open, mark it 'no mass properties'. Dual check: |V_brep−V_mesh|/V_brep ≤ 2·(4/3)δ/r_min+1e-6, with both positive. Mesh inputs: weld at 1e-6·bbox, drop degenerate/duplicate faces, fix winding, split into closed bodies, fill small holes, and run the interference check. (B) Mass. Material per part (3-D print effective density ρ_mat·[shell+infill·(1−shell)] is an estimate tier). m_i=ρ_iV_i, c_i, I_i from B-rep (mm⁵→m⁵ ×1e-15), composited with the full tensor. Measured overrides: mass-only uses uniform scaling (k=m_meas/m_cad, I←kI). Mass+CG uses the default residual lumped mass Δm=m_meas−m_cad at c_Δ=(m_meas c_meas−m_cad c_cad)/Δm, with a warning if Δm<0 or c_Δ falls outside the envelope; show both models. Transform with c_r=sRc+t and I_r=RIRᵀ·s² at fixed mass. (C) Fit. ## Risks

- OCP 8.x is new (OCCT 8.0.1) and breaks APIs used in most online examples (TopoDS casts, NCollection typedefs moved to OCP.collections, Bnd_Box.Get unbound, GK flags). Pin the exact version and keep all OCP calls behind one adapter with a regression test suite (the analytic fixtures from this research).
- Silent STEP corruption is possible even for files written by OCCT itself (badrevolve case: valid in memory, invalid after a STEP round trip, 7x volume). Without dual-path validation (BRepCheck plus B-rep-vs-mesh volume) wrong masses would be reported as 'exact'.
- cadquery-ocp pins vtk==9.6.2 exactly; the latest vtk is 9.7.1. Depending on cadquery-ocp instead of cadquery-ocp-novtk will create dependency conflicts with pyvista/VTK in the viewer.
- gmsh is GPL-2+ and loads in-process from Python. To keep the main app licence-clean it must run as a separate executable/process (file-based interface). Its curvature-based sizing can hang on singular points (nose apex) unless MeshSizeMin is set and runs are time-limited.
- Mesh booleans (manifold3d) on independently tessellated parts with coincident curved faces produce sliver channels: wrong topology (genus 9) and +1.75% wetted area even though the volume is right. Mesh-only inputs need topology checks (Euler characteristic, area consistency) and cannot reach B-rep quality.
- trimesh voxelization is a memory bomb (OOM at ≥11 GB for 1 mm pitch on a 250 mm part) and its OML is ±8-10% off. Do not use it except as an explicitly labelled estimate with memory guards.
- IGES and some STEP exports are surface models (no solids). Sewing may not close them, so mass properties are impossible. The UI must handle 'display-only geometry plus user-entered mass' gracefully.
- Mass accuracy is dominated by density assumptions (3-D-print infill, composite layups, glue, wiring), not by geometry. The UI must make density/measured-mass overrides prominent and show both override models.
- Alignment ambiguities: cylinder direction and roll cannot be inferred and need user input. Unit inference fails for parts whose size is not envelope-like (internal modules), so it needs confirmation. The 'dominant radius' heuristic is wrong for nose cones (use base radius/profile).
- Exact B-rep sectioning is slow-ish (5-12 s per 200 stations for simple parts) and B-rep fuse may be slow or fragile for large assemblies with thousands of fasteners. Mitigate by pre-culling internal parts by bounding-box containment before fusing, using a fuzzy value, parallelising per station, and caching.
- OpenRocket override semantics (unit inertia × overridden mass; .ork tag names overridemass/overridecg/overridesubcomponents*) are from recall and must be verified against OR 23.09/24.x source and files before implementing the .ork write-back.
- Wheel footprint: OCP is about 176 MB uncompressed, gmsh about 146 MB (about 104 MB after dropping the .lib), VTK about 77 MB compressed, so the installer will be several hundred MB. OCCT also prints to stdout, which must be silenced in a GUI/frozen app.

## Open questions

- Should replacing a component automatically supersede the OR subcomponents inside its axial span (mass components, bulkheads, centering rings), or always ask? What default for recovery devices whose mass leaves at deployment?
- How should a CAD part that spans several OR components be handled (e.g. a nose cone with an integrated shoulder and payload bay)? Map one CAD part to multiple OR components, or merge OR components first?
- Roll reference: which OR feature defines 0° roll (fin set 1, launch lug/rail button angle offset)? Should the user snap a picked CAD feature to it?
- What acceptance tolerance applies to diameter/length mismatch between the CAD part and the OR component (proposed: |ΔR| > max(0.1 mm, 0.25% R) warns), and should 'scale to fit' ever be offered?
- Material library: what effective densities apply to 3-D-printed parts (infill and walls) and composite layups, and will the user usually have a measured mass/CG to drive the residual-lumped-mass override?
- Will user CAD typically arrive as STEP assemblies with internal parts (B-rep path, exact) or as single STL exports (mesh path, unit inference needed)? This decides how much effort goes into mesh repair.
- Is IGES support required at all? It is cheap via OCP, but often surfaces-only.
- Exact .ork override tag names and OR's inertia treatment under mass override in 23.09/24.x need confirmation by the OpenRocket-integration researcher.
- Which parts are load-carrying for beam-stress sections (tube vs internal sled)? This needs a per-part 'structural' tag in the UI.
- Freezing/packaging: confirm PyInstaller or Nuitka handles OCP's 108 MB .pyd and the delvewheel'd .libs folder on Windows (GitHub Actions windows runner test), and decide whether the gmsh worker is a separate frozen Python exe or gmsh.exe from the SDK.
